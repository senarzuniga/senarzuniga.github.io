INGETRANS Digital Twin for High-Variability Job Mix

High-Mix, Short-Run Corrugated Plant Benchmark

This Digital Twin compares conventional forklift-based reel handling versus INGETRANS automated reel feeding under high job variability, frequent reel changes and short production runs. Both scenarios were simulated using identical production conditions and plant constraints.

Executive Summary

In short-run production, logistics continuity becomes production-critical. The simulation confirms that INGETRANS delivers a stronger advantage when the plant has frequent changeovers, high SKU variability and tight scheduling windows.

Strategic Conclusion

In high-mix, short-run production, the value of automation is continuity. INGETRANS keeps the next reel ready at the right roll stand at the right time, reducing starvation and preserving schedule adherence without increasing machine speed.
